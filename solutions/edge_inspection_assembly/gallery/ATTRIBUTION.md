## detection-overlay.jpg

Source file: `evaluation/runs/2026-09-05-m1-smoke/overlay_12300060_test.jpg`.

The detector's own output on one DeepPCB validation image (`12300060_test.jpg`,
group 12300), drawn by the smoke script from the ONNX model's detections — the
same frame whose ground-truth boxes were later used to generate the six-item
expected list in the shipped example configuration.

The underlying image comes from the **DeepPCB** dataset:

> DeepPCB — A PCB defect dataset for detection
> https://github.com/tangsanli5201/DeepPCB
> Licensed under the MIT License.

The MIT licence permits redistribution and commercial use and requires the
copyright notice to travel with copies and derivative works, which is what this
file is for. The images are binarised greyscale scans of bare PCB copper layers;
they contain no people and no customer-identifying content, so nothing is
obscured.

Note for anyone reusing this image on a page about assembly inspection: it shows
**bare-board copper defects**, not missing assembly parts. The caption in
`solution.yaml` says so, and the notice at the top of `description.md` says why.

## dimension-nominal.png / dimension-oversize.png

Source files: `evaluation/runs/2026-09-05-m1-smoke/dimension_nominal.png` and
`dimension_oversize_62mm.png`.

Both are synthetic scenes generated by `evaluation/make_dimension_fixture.py`
for this project — an ArUco marker (DICT_4X4_50) as the calibration reference
plus a rectangular target at a known size, rendered at a known
millimetres-per-pixel so the ground truth comes from the generation parameters
rather than from a second measurement. No third-party material is involved and
no attribution is required for them; they are listed here so the provenance of
every gallery asset is on one page.

The measurements drawn on them are the smoke run's real output: nominal case
60 x 40 mm measured as 60.241 x 40.261 mm (worst relative error 0.65% against a
1% budget, verdict `ok`); oversize case measured 62.25 mm against a 60 mm
nominal and 1.0 mm tolerance, verdict NG, Modbus HR 10 = 6225 and HR 11 = 2.
