## Gallery screenshots (2026-10-08)

`dashboard-zh-20261008.jpg` and `voice-light-on-zh-20261008.jpg` are real screenshots of Home Assistant 2026.9.3 (Apache License 2.0, https://www.home-assistant.io), image digest pinned by this package, running as a throwaway container on a Raspberry Pi 5 on 2026-10-08. Devices are screenshot-only simulations (template lights/switch/cover/sensors, generic_thermostat) on a hand-built dashboard; no physical devices. Speech-to-text and text-to-speech run on a Jetson Orin NX 16GB through the Wyoming Protocol integration.

The spoken command 把客厅灯打开 was synthesized by the same Jetson text-to-speech and fed to the browser as its microphone input; the Assist dialog sent it through the voice pipeline. No human recording or third-party audio was used. The right-hand tile in `voice-light-on-zh-20261008.jpg` is the living-room light card captured after the dialog closed. No credentials appear in the images.

`cover-20261008.jpg` is a generated illustration, not a screenshot.
