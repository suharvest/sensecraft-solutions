## What This Reference Design Is

**Rehabilitation posture tracking running entirely on edge cameras.**

The camera tracks 33 full-body joints and measures joint angles in real time.

When knee valgus or shoulder shrug occurs, the on-screen HUD alerts the user. Valid repetitions count automatically.

Vectors and metrics stream over local WebSocket to existing dashboards.

No video leaves the camera. Processing stays in local memory, protecting privacy offline.

**Limits:** The patient must stand or sit 2–3 m facing the camera. This is an assistive training tool, not a medical diagnosis device.
